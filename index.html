The problem

One coffee strength doesn't fit every hour.

We've all seen it: a student buys strong coffee late at night, struggles to sleep, and reaches for another strong coffee the next morning. Others avoid coffee entirely because a typical cup makes them feel jittery.

We started U Coffee because your energy needs change throughout the day. Your coffee should be able to change with them.

Our answer: the U Scale.

Choose a flavor you love and the caffeine level that fits the moment. The level is printed right on the can, and its color gets deeper as the caffeine goes up. No guessing.

More than a can

Your schedule meets your coffee.

Our companion app brings the U Scale to life. Tell it about your classes, caffeine tolerance, and sleep goals. Explore a suggested level for each part of the day, track your caffeine, and build an eight-can weekly box.

The app also includes a planned campus fridge finder, loyalty points, and student referrals. The prototype is already available to explore.

The road ahead

Built thoughtfully. Launched together.

Our next steps are to validate recipes and caffeine levels, get co-packer quotes, confirm campus distribution partnerships, finalize reward fulfillment, and launch the app. Our biggest dependencies are manufacturing cost, university approvals, and reliable delivery.

We'll share updates as we hit milestones, and we'll be clear when timelines or costs change. This is a concept-stage venture, so we want backers to understand the work still ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy U Coffee now?

Not yet. The website and app are prototypes, and this page is a Kickstarter campaign preview.

Where will U Coffee be sold?

Our proposed launch channels include campus fridges, vending machines, partner cafés, and weekly boxes. Specific locations require approval.

Why stop at 200 mg?

We want choices across a range, rather than making the strongest drink possible. Individual caffeine needs and sensitivities differ.

When will rewards ship?

A timeline has not been set. Production quotes and fulfillment plans need to be confirmed before launch.

Is this an official University of Miami product?

No. U Coffee is a student startup concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Miami.
